## Description

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 AI management system requirements. Covers AI policy, risk assessment, resource management, and performance evaluation for organizations deploying AI.

## Scoring dimensions

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| AI policy | Organizational AI governance policy |
| Risk assessment | AI-specific risk identification and evaluation |
| Resource management | Competence, awareness, and communication |
| Performance evaluation | Monitoring, measurement, and internal audit |
