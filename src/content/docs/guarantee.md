Cryptographically signed. Published on-chain. Permanent.

## The 6 Commitments

### 1. Core software free forever.

No feature gating. No "premium" features. The free version never feels incomplete.

### 2. Never collect, store, sell, or monetize user data.

The architecture makes this mathematically impossible, not just a policy promise.

### 3. Never insert ads, trackers, telemetry, or analytics.

Zero. If you run Brivora on an air-gapped machine, it works identically.

### 4. Financial operations always publicly verifiable.

All revenue on-chain. Public treasury wallet. Live dashboard.

### 5. Never acquired by a company that doesn't honor these commitments.

If the acquirer won't honor them, the acquisition doesn't happen. AGPL ensures the community can fork.

### 6. Never restrict access based on geography, politics, or identity.

Brivora is an equalizer.

## Enforcement mechanisms

**AGPL v3** -- The license enforces open source. The community can fork if any commitment is broken.

**On-chain publication** -- The Guarantee is published to Arweave. It cannot be edited or deleted. It is permanent and verifiable by anyone, forever.

**Cryptographic signature** -- Signed with the founder's PGP key. The signature proves the commitment was made by the founder and has not been tampered with.
