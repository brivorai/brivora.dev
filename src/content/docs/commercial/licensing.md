Brivora packages are licensed under **AGPL-3.0-or-later**. This means:

- **Open source projects:** Free forever. No restrictions.
- **Proprietary SaaS/products:** If you modify Brivora or serve it as part of a hosted service, AGPL requires you to publish your source code. If you cannot or prefer not to do that, you need a commercial license.

## FAQ

**Can I try before I buy?**
Yes. The AGPL-licensed packages are fully functional. Use them freely during development and evaluation. You only need a commercial license when deploying in a proprietary production environment.

**What if my company grows past a revenue tier?**
Upgrade at renewal. No retroactive charges.

**Do governance packs require a commercial license?**
No. Governance packs work with both AGPL and commercial licenses. They are data (JSON rule definitions), not software modifications.

**Can I get a refund?**
Yes, within 30 days of purchase if you haven't deployed to production.
